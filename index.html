Speed-to-Lead Automation

Solara Solar

A speed-to-lead system that calls new solar enquiries, qualifies their needs and books a phone consultation or appointment.

Explore the project ＋

The Challenge

Follow up new solar enquiries promptly and turn an initial expression of interest into a qualified conversation and a booked next step.

The Solution

When a visitor submits the solar enquiry form with consent, n8n records the lead in Airtable and initiates an AI callback. The assistant discusses their solar requirements, qualifies the enquiry and helps book a phone consultation or appointment. Call results and follow-up needs are recorded for the sales team.

Key Capabilities

  • Website enquiry capture with validation and call consent
  • AI callback triggered by a new form submission
  • Conversational qualification of solar requirements
  • Phone consultation or appointment booking
  • Airtable lead records, call outcomes and sales follow-up routing

Technology Stack

HTML / CSS / JavaScript / Node.js / n8n / Airtable / Retell AI / Twilio

Independent portfolio project for a fictional solar business. The project includes lead capture, AI callback and consultation or appointment booking. No guaranteed response time, quantified conversion result or production client deployment is claimed.

Lead Automation

Roofline

Lead automation for roofing enquiries, qualification and organised follow-up.

Explore the project ＋

The Challenge

Capture roofing enquiries consistently and give the business enough context to qualify and follow up each lead.

The Solution

Built a roofing lead-automation entry point with validated enquiry forms and server-side forwarding to n8n. The payload carries service, property, urgency and contact preferences for downstream qualification. A separate read-only dashboard presents enquiry, appointment and SMS snapshots for review.

Key Capabilities

  • Roofing-specific enquiry capture with contact and postcode validation
  • Structured qualification fields for service, damage, property and timing
  • Server-side n8n webhook forwarding with clear submission feedback
  • Read-only enquiry, booking and SMS dashboard with JSON snapshot import
  • Prepared receptionist knowledge and booking rules for follow-up integration

Technology Stack

HTML / CSS / JavaScript / Node.js / n8n webhook integration / JSON

Independent portfolio project for a fictional roofing business. The dashboard reads sample data or imported snapshots rather than live monitoring. Prepared receptionist and booking rules do not establish an active production calling or booking service.
