A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is my home suitable for solar panels?

Roof size, orientation, condition and shading all matter. A qualified installer would assess your property before recommending a system. This demonstration doesn't assess roof suitability.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days?

Solar panels can generate electricity from daylight even when it's cloudy. Output varies with weather, season, shading and the system itself.

Do I need a battery with solar panels?

A battery is optional. It stores surplus electricity for later use, but whether it suits your home depends on your consumption, system and budget.

What happens when I submit the form?

Your enquiry is saved through n8n to our demonstration Airtable base. With consent, the workflow then requests an immediate AI call through Retell and Twilio, 24/7. The on-screen confirmation confirms the save; a successful call depends on the calling services. Email follow-up is not connected.

Is Solara a real solar installation company?

No. Solara is a fictional brand created for an AI automation portfolio. No installation services, quotations or appointments are offered through this demo.
